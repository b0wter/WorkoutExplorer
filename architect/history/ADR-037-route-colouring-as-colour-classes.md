## Rev. 1 (7 Nov 2026, with F-25 v0.2)

The class count was raised as this ADR's own "would choose differently" foresaw. Superseded text:

- Decision 1: "Each sample's value maps to one of a fixed small number of **colour classes** (F-25: 7), or to none (missing)."
- Would choose differently if: "The operator finds 7 steps too coarse on screen: first raise the class count (up to about 12, still one feature per class), before considering per-edge features."
